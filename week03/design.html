Web Design Principles

💡 "Design for the user and your business objectives." – Peep Laja

Core Concepts to Remember

Visual Hierarchy

Establish a clear hierarchy of information to help users understand the relative importance of different elements on the page. Use techniques like size, color, and typography to emphasize important content and guide the user's eye naturally through the layout.

Accessibility Focus

Ensure that your design is accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. This involves providing alternative text for images, using sufficient color contrast, and implementing keyboard navigation. Test your work with tools like WAVE or Lighthouse.

Key Principles

  • Typography: Max 2-3 fonts. Include only needed weights.
  • Colors: Limit palette to 5 hues or less. Test contrast.
  • Clarity: Clean, uncluttered layout with ample whitespace.
  • Consistency: Maintain a consistent visual language.
  • Feedback: Provide clear cues for user interactions.
